# NR carrier aggregation live check — 2026-11-25

## Device and test

- Device: Sony Xperia 5 IV SOG09, KDDI firmware `64.2.D.2.261`, baseband `waipio.gen-10361-55`.
- SIM/network: Yes, PLMN `502-152`.
- Serving cell during capture: NR SA n28, ARFCN 152670, PCI 238.
- Test: Wi-Fi disabled temporarily; one 25,000,000-byte Cloudflare download completed with HTTP 200 in 19.4998 s. Effective rate was about 10.26 Mbit/s. This is a single sample, not a controlled benchmark; VPN/app-path and server/load effects were not isolated.
- Capture: 60-second Qualcomm DIAG RRC capture while the download ran. The raw trace is intentionally not published because modem logs can expose subscriber, location and network metadata.

## Direct observation

The trace decoded as NR RRC version 17 and contained 13 NR RRC message packets on the n28 serving cell. It included repeated UE measurement reports, one downlink `RRCReconfiguration` removing a measurement configuration, and the corresponding `RRCReconfigurationComplete`. No `sCellToAddModList` or `sCellToReleaseList` appeared in this capture. Therefore, the network did not configure an NR secondary cell in the observed traffic-loaded interval.

The reports included a weaker neighbor cell (PCI 197), but the captured measurement configuration did not identify that neighbor's band. It cannot be asserted to be n78 from PCI or RSRP alone. The user reports little or no usable n78 at home; that is consistent with the network not adding an n78 SCell, but the capture alone cannot prove why the network omitted it.

A separate earlier trace on this device contained an n78 SCell configuration followed by `RRCReconfigurationComplete`. This proves that a network previously sent an n78 SCell configuration and the UE completed the RRC procedure. It does not prove that the SCell was activated at MAC or carried user data.

## Why Android status is inconclusive

During the live audit, Android reported `isUsingCarrierAggregation=false`, empty `mCellBandwidths`, and an NR `PhysicalChannelConfig` with band and bandwidth set to zero and an invalid channel sentinel. The SOG09 RIL drops NR channel metadata, so these Android fields are not a reliable CA detector. RRC/DIAG evidence is stronger, but a complete active-CA proof also needs MAC activation and per-carrier scheduling/traffic counters.

## EFS state and change decision

- `/nv/item_files/modem/nr5g/RRC/cap_limit_bw` and its subscription copy remain 816 bytes. Byte `0x4C` is `0A` in both, preserving the prior n28 20 MHz allowance. This field limits bandwidth on a carrier; it is not an NR CA enable.
- Live `/mdb/nr/plmn2cacombos_nr.mdb` remains the stock K4 payload. Its decoded strings contain n28+n78 and n78+n78 combinations, but exact PLMN gating and applicability to the current SIM are not established.
- The inspected explicit `cap_control_nrca_*` files are absent. That absence does not mean NR CA is disabled. MR-DC control fields are present but their schema/semantics are not validated as NR CA switches.
- This capture did not include a fresh UE capability information response. Earlier filtered capability evidence listed n28+n78 and n78+n78; a fresh full response is still needed.
- No EFS or modem profile was written in this check. Wi-Fi was restored; the capture verified restoration of the original DIAG logging mask; root stayed active; no reboot occurred.

**Conclusion:** there is no verified phone-side NR CA blocker to patch from this evidence. The loaded test showed no SCell configured, and n78 coverage is reportedly poor. Do not write an undocumented EFS byte, alter `cap_limit_bw` as a CA switch, or import another model's MBN. Re-test where n78 is confirmed usable before considering any device-side profile investigation.

## Reusable worldwide workflow

This method is useful on other 5G NR SA devices, but device profile bytes, RF combinations, band support and carrier policy are not portable.

1. Record exact model/SKU, software build, modem revision, active SIM slot/PLMN and MBN. Start with automatic RAT/band selection and confirm cellular—not Wi-Fi—is carrying the test.
2. Record serving-cell and candidate-cell band, ARFCN, bandwidth, PCI and signal. Confirm the intended second band is receivable; a 5G icon is not evidence of CA.
3. Capture the current UE capability exchange. Inspect `supportedBandCombinationList` and linked feature sets for the exact NR band pair, bandwidth and DL/UL layer support. Account for network-filtered capability responses.
4. During sustained cellular download, capture modem RRC. In `RRCReconfiguration`, inspect `sCellToAddModList`, each SCell's identity/frequency/bandwidth/BWP and `sCellState`, then verify the matching `RRCReconfigurationComplete`.
5. RRC configuration is not enough to prove active CA. Check SCell activation/deactivation MAC CE or the explicitly activated state, followed by per-carrier PDCCH/PDSCH and scheduler counters under traffic.
6. Treat Android CA/physical-channel state as corroboration only when the OEM RIL exposes valid values.
7. If the UE does not advertise the pair, identify the exact device, RF, firmware/MBN or capability-filter limit. If the UE advertises it but the network sends no SCell configuration under good signal and load, investigate site deployment, operator policy and subscription provisioning. If configured but inactive, inspect MAC activation/timers and scheduler behavior.
8. Modify modem files only after a specific live record is proven to gate a supported pair on that exact device and firmware. Back up and hash the original, change one understood field, read back byte-for-byte, validate and retain a rollback. Never copy a regional MBN or undocumented EFS offset between models.

## Standards

- [3GPP TS 38.306 — UE radio access capabilities](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/138300_138399/138306/16.05.00_60/ts_138306v160500p.pdf): UE band-combination and feature-set capability.
- [3GPP TS 38.331 — NR RRC](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/138300_138399/138331/18.08.00_60/ts_138331v180800p.pdf): SCell addition/modification in `RRCReconfiguration`.
- [3GPP TS 38.321 — NR MAC](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/138300_138399/138321/16.04.00_60/ts_138321v160400p.pdf): SCell activation/deactivation at MAC.
